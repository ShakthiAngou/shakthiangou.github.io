Project · May 2026

Microgrid Energy Management System

A modular simulation for coordinating solar generation, load demand, supercapacitor storage, and hydrogen storage across 24-hour microgrid scenarios.

GitHub Repository

Purpose

The long-term goal is to develop an intelligent energy management system for decentralised microgrids in rural India, supporting reliable access to renewable energy. The current simulation provides a foundation for testing dispatch strategies before moving toward physical hardware.

System

Solar Generation

Models renewable energy supplied to the microgrid over a daily operating scenario.

Load Demand

Tracks energy consumption and the balance between generation, storage, and demand.

Supercapacitor Storage

Handles short-duration energy storage as the first priority in the dispatch strategy.

Hydrogen Storage

Provides a pathway for longer-duration storage after short-term capacity is used.

Current Implementation

  • Rule-based dispatch prioritizes short-duration storage before hydrogen conversion.
  • Energy flows are tracked across configurable 24-hour scenarios.
  • A Matplotlib dashboard presents storage state of charge, hydrogen inventory, generation, demand, and the post-storage energy balance.

Roadmap

Future versions will work toward forecast-aware model predictive control, hardware-in-the-loop testing, and ML-based predictive state estimation for more adaptive energy management.
